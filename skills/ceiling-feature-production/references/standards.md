# Trust-Lines standard sizes

Defaults to **propose** when a render or brief gives no number. They are never
facts: every value used from here goes into the part's `source.estimated` and
gets confirmed by the designer.

**Status: provisional.** Every value below comes from one job (435, July 2026).
The office should confirm or correct each row and add its real standards. When a
new job is added to `examples/`, update the "Seen in" column and the
confidence.

Units: cm.

| Item | Default | Seen in | Confidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Band thickness | 5.0 | 435 (both rings) | one job |
| Plain band height | 10.0 | 435 decorative chandelier | one job |
| Lit band height | 20.0 | 435 chandelier with light | one job |
| Outer corner radius | R15.0 (inner R10.0 at 5 thick) | 435 (all corners) | one job |
| Gap between nested rings | about 15 in plan | 435 sheet 1 (15.0 each side) | one job |
| Typical size | 100 to 145 per face | 435 | one job |
