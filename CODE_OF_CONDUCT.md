# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Be respectful, constructive, and welcoming. Discuss technical decisions without personal attacks, harassment, discrimination, or intimidation. Respect privacy and never post another person's proprietary drawings, credentials, or personal information.

## Scope

This standard applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and other project spaces.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the repository owner. The maintainers may edit or remove content and temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.
